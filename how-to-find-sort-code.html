How to Find Your Sort Code

Your sort code is a 6-digit number that identifies your bank and branch in the UK. It is usually easy to find once you know where to look.

1. On your bank card

Many debit cards display the sort code on the front, often alongside your account number.

2. On your bank statement

Your sort code will always appear on printed or digital bank statements.

3. In your banking app

Most UK banking apps show your sort code clearly in your account details section.

4. Online banking

If you log into your bank’s website, your sort code is usually listed with your account information.
